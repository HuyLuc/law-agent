## Loi / can thiep trong qua trinh chay bo test

1. **Chay V0-V3 gop chung (`--versions v0,v1,v2,v3`) bi kill sau 2 gio** —
   script chi ghi file ket qua SAU KHI toan bo 1 phien ban chay xong (khong
   co checkpoint giua chung), nen toan bo tien do bi mat (khong co phien
   ban nao duoc luu). Nguyen nhan: quota Gemini (25 to hop key x model) can
   kiet nhanh hon nhieu so voi bo dev (100 cau x 4 phien ban = gap 2.5 lan
   so luong goi), dan den chay cham do roi xuong Groq lien tuc.
   -> **Khac phuc**: chia nho, chay TUNG phien ban mot (`--out-suffix
   _v0only`, `_v1only`, ...) thay vi gop chung, de neu bi kill giua chung
   it nhat con giu duoc cac phien ban da xong.

2. **Groq tra ve loi `json_validate_failed`** (model sinh JSON sai dinh
   dang, vi du thieu dau ngoac dong chuoi) khi cham diem giam khao
   (`judge_answer`), xay ra dung luc TAT CA 25 to hop Gemini da chet trong
   ngay -> khong con fallback nao, lam sap toan bo tien trinh ngay lap tuc.
   -> **Khac phuc**: them `_invoke_groq_with_retry()` trong `src/llm.py`
   (thu lai toi da 3 lan, cach nhau 3s) cho rieng loi goi Groq, vi day la
   tuyen cuoi cung khi Gemini da het sach — 1 lan loi thoang qua khong nen
   lam hong ca batch nhieu gio chay. Da chay lai full test suite (71 pass)
   sau khi sua.

3. **Docker Desktop khong chay** (loi ket noi `dockerDesktopLinuxEngine`)
   sau khi phien lam viec bi gian doan qua dem — container `qdrant` (giu
   du lieu that, dung xuyen suot du an) khong truy cap duoc.
   -> **Khac phuc**: khoi dong lai Docker Desktop, cho ~5s roi `docker
   start qdrant`, kiem tra `curl http://localhost:6333/collections` tra ve
   dung collection `labor_law` — du lieu con nguyen.

4. **Bug reset quota theo ngay khong hoat dong dung khi tien trinh chay
   xuyen qua nua dem** — `_dead_gemini_keys` (bo nho cac to hop Gemini da
   het quota trong ngay) chi duoc nap 1 lan luc import module
   (`src/llm.py`). Neu 1 lan chay keo dai qua nhieu gio va bang qua moc
   nua dem, cac to hop da chet TU HOM TRUOC bi ghi nham thanh chet CUA
   NGAY MOI khi `_save_dead_keys()` duoc goi lai (vi ham nay luon dung
   `datetime.now()` hien tai de ghi ngay, nhung danh sach dead_indices
   trong bo nho van la cua ngay cu, khong tu xoa).
   -> **Khac phuc**: them `_refresh_dead_keys_if_new_day()`, goi o dau moi
   `invoke()` de tu dong reset bo nho khi phat hien sang ngay moi. Da xoa
   file `.gemini_dead_keys.json` cu (du lieu bi nhiem bug, khong dang tin)
   de bat dau lai sach cho ngay 2026-10-05. Da chay lai full test suite
   (71 pass) sau khi sua.

5. **V0 tren bo test bi kill 2 lan lien tiep (2 gio/lan, tong ~4 gio) ma
   van chua xong 100 cau** — vi `run_generation_eval.py` chi ghi file KHI
   TOAN BO 1 phien ban chay xong (khong co checkpoint giua chung), nen moi
   lan bi kill la mat trang, phai chay lai tu cau dau tien. Day la nguyen
   nhan goc khien V0 khong bao gio hoan thanh duoc trong 1 lan chay 2 gio.
   -> **Khac phuc**: viet lai `evaluate_version()` de GHI FILE SAU MOI CAU
   HOI (khong doi het phien ban), va `main()` tu dong **resume**: neu file
   ket qua da co san voi mot so cau da xong (parse tu `per_item`), bo qua
   cac id da xong, chi chay tiep cac cau con thieu. Da smoke-test: chay 1
   cau, kill gia lap (chay lai lenh), xac nhan lan 2 bao "da xong tu
   truoc, bo qua" thay vi goi lai LLM. Full test suite van pass (71 pass)
   sau khi sua.

## Trang thai hien tai (dang cap nhat)

- Dang chay **V0** tren bo test (100 cau), `--out-suffix _v0only`.
- Ke hoach: V0 -> V1 -> V2 -> V3 -> V4, moi phien ban mot file rieng, gop
  lai vao `eval/results/generation_test.json` sau khi tat ca xong (giong
  cach da lam voi V4 o bo dev).
- **Dieu kien dung (nguoi dung xac nhan)**: dung lai ngay khi quota
  **Gemini** can het (ca 25 to hop key x model deu chet trong ngay), KHONG
  grind tiep bang Groq mot minh nhu da lam o bo dev (lan do ton nhieu gio
  cho rolling-window cua Groq). Khi dung, ghi ro vao file nay: phien ban
  nao da xong, phien ban nao dang do, va trang thai dead-keys luc dung.
- **Khi dung lai (theo yeu cau nguoi dung)**: tu dong (1) `git commit`
  toan bo thay doi dang do (ket qua da chay duoc, file log nay, moi sua
  doi code lien quan), (2) tat cac container Docker dang chay (`docker
  compose down` / `docker stop qdrant`), (3) dua may tinh vao che do sleep.
